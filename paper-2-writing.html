Paper 2 Roadmap

What Students Must Master

Paper 2 success depends on format, audience awareness, strong ideas, paragraph control and language accuracy.

📘

Directed Writing

Write for a specific form, audience and purpose.

  • Email, letter, report, article and speech.
  • Correct register and tone.
  • Use task details meaningfully.
✍️

Descriptive Writing

Create atmosphere and vivid detail.

  • Use sensory detail.
  • Control imagery.
  • Avoid turning description into story.
🎯

Narrative Writing

Build a controlled story.

  • Create conflict and development.
  • Use character motivation.
  • End with impact.
✨

Language Accuracy

Write with grammar and punctuation control.

  • Check tense and agreement.
  • Use varied sentences.
  • Proofread final answer.
🧠

Planning

Plan before writing.

  • Decode task first.
  • Decide paragraph order.
  • Know your ending before you begin.
📌

A* Style

Write with maturity and purpose.

  • Use precise vocabulary.
  • Avoid repetition.
  • Maintain tone consistently.

A* Writing Method

The Paper 2 Success Method

Good writing is planned, purposeful and proofread.

1

Decode F-A-P-T

Identify format, audience, purpose and tone.

2

Plan Paragraphs

Decide the function of each paragraph before writing.

3

Develop Ideas

Do not list points; explain, illustrate and connect them.

4

Control Style

Match vocabulary and sentence style to the task.

5

Proofread Carefully

Correct grammar, punctuation, spelling and paragraphing.

Weak vs Strong Writing

How A* Writing Looks Different

Strong writing is not just longer. It is more controlled and appropriate.

Weak Student Habits

  • Writes without planning.
  • Uses the wrong format.
  • Mixes formal and informal tone.
  • Uses repeated vocabulary.
  • Leaves careless grammar errors.

A* Student Habits

  • Plans form and paragraph order.
  • Uses audience-appropriate register.
  • Develops ideas with detail.
  • Uses varied sentence structures.
  • Proofreads before finishing.

Teacher Tip

Before writing any Paper 2 answer, ask: What am I writing, who am I writing to, why am I writing, and what tone should I use?
