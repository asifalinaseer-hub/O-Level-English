Core Notes

Why Language Accuracy Matters

Good ideas lose marks if grammar, punctuation or vocabulary are careless. Accuracy helps the examiner trust the writing.

🎯

Grammar Control

  • Use correct tense and subject-verb agreement.
  • Avoid sentence fragments and run-ons.
  • Check pronouns and articles.
🧠

Punctuation

  • Use full stops, commas and apostrophes accurately.
  • Avoid comma splices.
  • Use dialogue punctuation correctly in narratives.
✍️

Vocabulary and Style

  • Choose precise words, not over-complicated words.
  • Avoid repetition.
  • Match vocabulary to audience and purpose.
CIE-Aligned Skill Map

Accuracy Toolkit

These are the main areas students must control.

Sentence Boundaries

  • Every sentence must be complete.
  • Avoid joining two sentences with only a comma.

Tense Control

  • Stay consistent unless time shifts clearly.
  • Check narratives carefully.

Agreement

  • Subject and verb must match.
  • Check singular/plural nouns.

Commas

  • Use commas to separate clauses or items.
  • Do not insert commas randomly.

Apostrophes

  • Use for possession and contraction.
  • Do not use for ordinary plurals.

Vocabulary

  • Use precise words.
  • Avoid forced difficult words.

Paragraphing

  • New idea, new paragraph.
  • Use paragraphs to control reader movement.

Proofreading

  • Read the answer silently before finishing.
  • Correct obvious errors.
A* Method

The Accuracy Improvement Method

Accuracy improves when students edit deliberately.

1

Write Clearly First

  • Do not sacrifice clarity for fancy vocabulary.
  • Simple accurate writing is better than confused complex writing.
2

Check Sentence Ends

  • Every sentence needs a complete idea.
  • Fix fragments and run-ons.
3

Check Verbs

  • Look for tense errors.
  • Check subject-verb agreement.
4

Check Punctuation

  • Add full stops where needed.
  • Remove unnecessary commas.
5

Replace Repetition

  • Use varied vocabulary carefully.
  • Avoid repeating the same adjective.
6

Final Proofread

  • Read line by line.
  • Correct spelling, capitals and paragraphing.
Answer Formula

C-P-V-S Formula

Use this quick final check before submitting writing.

C

Clarity

  • Can the reader understand every sentence?
  • Remove confusing wording.
P

Punctuation

  • Check full stops, commas and apostrophes.
  • Fix dialogue punctuation if used.
V

Vocabulary

  • Replace weak repeated words.
  • Keep vocabulary natural and appropriate.
S

Style

  • Match audience and purpose.
  • Maintain tone consistently.

Practice Plan

Practise Accuracy Every Week

Accuracy comes from correction, not just writing more.

Weekly Accuracy Practice

Train small accuracy habits consistently.

Day 1 Correct ten sentence boundary errors.
Day 2 Practise commas and full stops.
Day 3 Revise subject-verb agreement.
Day 4 Rewrite weak vocabulary into precise wording.
Day 5 Proofread one old essay carefully.
Day 6 Write a clean paragraph with zero careless errors.
A* language is not always complicated. It is accurate, controlled, varied and appropriate.
