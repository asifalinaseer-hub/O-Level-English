Core Notes

What Summary Writing Really Tests

A summary is not a shortened story. It is a controlled selection of relevant points written clearly and briefly.

🎯

Selection

  • Choose only points that answer the summary question.
  • Ignore background, examples, repetition and description unless required.
  • Underline useful information before writing.
🧠

Compression

  • Combine related points into one sentence.
  • Remove repeated ideas and unnecessary adjectives.
  • Write meaning, not decoration.
✍️

Own Words

  • Change wording without changing meaning.
  • Use simple accurate synonyms.
  • Do not copy whole phrases unless unavoidable.
CIE-Aligned Skill Map

Summary Skills Students Need

The best summaries are focused, concise and organised.

Read the Task

  • Identify exactly what to summarise.
  • Look for the focus word in the question.

Find Points

  • Mark only relevant points.
  • Separate main points from examples.

Avoid Storytelling

  • Do not retell the whole passage.
  • Do not include personal opinion.

Group Ideas

  • Put similar points together.
  • Create a logical order.

Condense

  • Replace long phrases with shorter wording.
  • Use one sentence for two related ideas.

Use Own Words

  • Paraphrase accurately.
  • Do not distort the meaning.

Stay Within Limit

  • Check word count.
  • Cut repeated or weak words.

Proofread

  • Check grammar, spelling and clarity.
  • Make every sentence earn marks.
A* Method

The 7-Step Summary Method

Use this method every time before writing the final summary.

1

Decode the Question

  • Circle the exact focus of the summary.
  • Ask: what kind of information is required?
2

Read the Relevant Text

  • Read for meaning first.
  • Do not pick points randomly.
3

Underline Possible Points

  • Mark all useful ideas lightly.
  • Avoid examples and repeated details.
4

Number the Best Points

  • Choose the strongest relevant points.
  • Prefer clear content points over vague ideas.
5

Group and Sequence

  • Put points in a logical order.
  • Avoid jumping around the passage unnecessarily.
6

Write Concisely

  • Use compact sentences.
  • Combine related ideas smoothly.
7

Edit Ruthlessly

  • Remove repetition.
  • Check word limit and accuracy.
Answer Formula

S-E-C Formula

Use this simple formula to build a strong summary habit.

S

Select

  • Pick only the information demanded by the question.
  • Ignore attractive but irrelevant details.
E

Express

  • Use your own words where possible.
  • Keep the original meaning exact.
C

Condense

  • Use fewer words without losing meaning.
  • Combine ideas and remove repetition.

Practice Plan

Build Summary Skill Gradually

Summary improves through selection and rewriting practice.

Weekly Summary Practice Plan

Use this schedule for disciplined practice.

Day 1 Underline relevant points in one passage only.
Day 2 Remove examples and repeated details from selected points.
Day 3 Rewrite ten phrases in your own words.
Day 4 Combine related points into fewer sentences.
Day 5 Write one full 150-word summary.
Day 6 Edit word count, grammar and relevance.
A summary is not a place to impress with fancy vocabulary. It is a place to show accurate selection, clear meaning and controlled expression.
