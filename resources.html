Resource Policy

How to Keep Resources Useful

Resources should be focused, original and student-friendly.

Best Resource Types

  • One-page checklists.
  • Short worksheets.
  • Model answer comparisons.
  • Vocabulary banks.
  • Timed practice sheets.

Avoid

  • Uploading copyrighted books.
  • Overloading students with huge files.
  • Unverified paper predictions.
  • Messy worksheets without answers.
  • Resources without teaching purpose.

Teacher Tip

The best resource is not the longest one. It is the one students can actually use, check and improve from.
