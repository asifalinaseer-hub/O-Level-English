Paper 1 Reading

Build Strong Reading and Summary Skills

Paper 1 preparation focuses on understanding passages, identifying meaning, analysing language and writing controlled answers.

Comprehension

Understand explicit and implicit meaning in passages.

Writer’s Effect

Analyse powerful words, phrases, imagery and effects.

Summary Writing

Select key points and write concisely in your own words.

Short Response

Answer clearly with precise evidence and explanation.

Directed Writing

Learn email, letter, report, article and speech formats.

Descriptive Writing

Create atmosphere, imagery and sensory detail.

Narrative Writing

Language Accuracy

Target the Exact Weak Areas

01

Inference Skills

Read between the lines and understand implied meaning.

02

Own Words Technique

03

Vocabulary Building

Use precise, mature and context-appropriate words.

04

Paragraph Control

Write organised, connected and exam-friendly paragraphs.

05
