Past Papers

Practise with a Smart System

Past papers should not be solved randomly. Students need a system: understand the question type, attempt the answer, compare with expectations, correct mistakes and rewrite improved responses.

Past Paper Method

  1. Attempt the question honestly.
  2. Underline what the question demands.
  3. Check relevance and evidence.
  4. Compare weak and strong answers.
  5. Rewrite the improved answer.
